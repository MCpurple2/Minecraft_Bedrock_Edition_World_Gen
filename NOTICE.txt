Moderner Beta
Copyright (c) 2024-2025 BlueStaggo, 2021 B3spectacled.

The world customization screens adapt Moderner Beta's layout, preset list,
navigation, settings actions and JSON editor. The finite world boundary
adapts WorldBorderLocation and the PRE out-of-bounds biome injection rule.

Build 3.8 adapts ModernBetaBiomePreviewScreen, the Voronoi magnifier's
LayerRandom sampler and the registered preview colors in SettingsComponentTypes
from revision 87c015b63e8618795dbeafe41166a3818eb81f8f. The port connects
the preview to the selected PE biome source and finite world boundary, and
adapts client texture management and PNG capture to Fabric 1.21.11.
These adaptations use the MIT-licensed upstream revision. The full copyright
notice, permission grant and disclaimer are in LICENSES/Moderner-Beta-MIT.txt.
