PE Worldgen: experimental PE 0.11.1 research port

Fabric adapter written for this project. Moderner Beta was consulted for its
separation of historical generation from modern Minecraft integration; it is
not a runtime dependency. No Moderner Beta implementation was copied into
this adapter. The Gradle wrapper launcher is from the Moderner Beta repository
(Gradle's Apache-2.0-licensed wrapper).

This project does not bundle the Minecraft Pocket Edition APK or native library.
See the core source and research notes for the actual reconstruction status.
